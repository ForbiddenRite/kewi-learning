Frequently Asked Questions

Can I retake an exercise?

No. Each student has only one attempt per exercise. Once submitted, answers cannot be modified.

How are scores calculated?

Each question is worth a fixed number of points (default 10). Your percentage is (correct answers ÷ total questions) × 100.

Who can create student accounts?

Only teachers can create, edit and manage student accounts from the Teacher Dashboard.

Is there a mobile version?

The platform is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops.

What does “Kewi” mean?

“Kewi” is formed from the initials of the English names of the platform’s developers. They also noticed it works as a playful misspelling of “kiwi,” which fits the fruit-inspired theme of the site.
