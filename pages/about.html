About Kewi Learning

Why the name “Kewi”? Because knowledge should be as fresh, nutritious, and delightful as a kiwi fruit.

Our Mission

Kewi Learning is an interactive online platform built for secondary students and their teachers. We believe learning should be engaging, measurable, and scalable — so new ideas can be added without rewriting everything from scratch.

Inspired by other online learning platforms, we expand beyond English into Mathematics, Science, History and General Knowledge, while keeping a clean, student-friendly experience.

🥝

Grow Like a Kiwi

Small on the outside, packed with goodness inside.

Design Principles

🌱 Scalable

Modular architecture, CSS variables and JS constants make it easy to add new exercise types, subjects or features.

🎨 Attractive

Kiwi-inspired colour palette, smooth interactions and clear visual hierarchy keep students engaged.

📐 Clean Code

No magic numbers. All static values live in constants and :root so global adjustments are one-line changes.

Who It’s For

Teachers

  • Create exercises (reading, MCQ, fill-blank, true/false…)
  • Manage student accounts
  • View performance reports and completion status
  • Monitor class progress at a glance

Students

  • Browse available exercises by subject & difficulty
  • Complete exercises with a focused, one-attempt design
  • See instant scores and detailed feedback
  • Compare progress on leaderboards
