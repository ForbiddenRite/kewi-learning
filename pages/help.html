Help Centre

For Students

  1. Log in with the credentials provided by your teacher.
  2. Browse available exercises on your dashboard.
  3. Select an exercise and complete all questions.
  4. Submit once — you cannot change answers afterwards.
  5. View your score and check the leaderboard.

For Teachers

  1. Log in with your teacher account.
  2. Create new exercises with the exercise builder.
  3. Add or manage student accounts.
  4. Review performance reports and completion status.
  5. Monitor class progress from the dashboard.
