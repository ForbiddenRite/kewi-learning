Platform Features

Everything you need for interactive, trackable online learning.

📝 Exercise Builder

Teachers can create multiple exercise types: Reading Comprehension (with sticky side-by-side layout), Multiple Choice, Fill-in-the-Blank, True/False and more. Each exercise supports difficulty levels and subject tags.

✅ Instant Auto-Grading

Once a student submits, answers are checked automatically. Scores and detailed feedback are available immediately to both student and teacher.

🔒 One-Attempt Policy

Students may attempt each exercise only once. This encourages careful reading and genuine understanding rather than trial-and-error.

📊 Performance Dashboards

Teachers see completion rates, average scores and individual student reports. Students can review their own results and class leaderboards.

👥 Account Management

Full CRUD for student accounts. Teachers create, edit, activate or deactivate student profiles with class assignment.

🎨 Reading-Friendly Layout

For reading comprehension, the passage stays sticky on one side while questions appear on the other — no more scrolling back and forth.

🏆 Leaderboards

Friendly competition with per-exercise and overall rankings. Motivates students while giving teachers insight into class performance.

🌓 Dark Mode & Responsive

Works beautifully on desktop and mobile. Toggle dark mode for comfortable late-night studying.
